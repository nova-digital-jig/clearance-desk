Before a buyer security questionnaire becomes a scramble, verify these seven facts.

For a 30–200-person B2B SaaS company that already holds a current SOC 2 or ISO 27001 report and has a live, named buyer questionnaire. This is not a readiness, certification, audit, legal, or security-control checklist.

1. Name the live questionnaire

Identify the buyer and the exact questionnaire file already in progress. A generic expectation that a questionnaire may arrive later is not enough to scope drafting work.

2. Count the questions

Confirm the file has 100 questions or fewer. A larger questionnaire needs a separate scope before work is accepted.

3. Confirm the report you already hold

Clearance Desk is only for a company that already has a current SOC 2 or ISO 27001 report. It is not a path to first-time certification or readiness.

4. Inventory evidence you are authorized to share

List existing policies, the report you hold, architecture notes, and relevant screenshots. Do not provide production credentials, private keys, buyer-portal passwords, government identifiers, or material the company is not authorized to share.

5. Name the company reviewer

A founder, CTO, CISO, counsel, or another authorized company reviewer must own the final answers and submission. A drafting service cannot sign representations for the company.

6. Preserve unknowns

If the supplied evidence does not support an answer, write unknown / not evidenced. Do not turn a missing control, missing document, or uncertain fact into a guessed “yes.”

7. Separate drafting from submission

The output can be an evidence-mapped draft answer packet, gap list, and reuse index. The company reviewer approves it and submits it. Buyer acceptance is not guaranteed.

When a five-business-day clock can begin

A clock starts only after payment is verified, the questionnaire is named, its file and authorized evidence pack are complete, a reviewer is named, and a delivery slot is free. Today collection is disabled, so no clock can start.

Choose the next step honestly

  • If there is no current SOC 2 or ISO 27001 report, seek the appropriate readiness or certification support elsewhere.
  • If there is no live named questionnaire, keep this checklist and return when one exists.
  • If the questionnaire exceeds 100 questions, stop and re-scope it.
  • If all seven facts are verified, review the exact Clearance Desk offer and exclusions.
