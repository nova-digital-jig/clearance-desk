Build an answerable evidence pack without turning the handoff into a secrets dump.

For a company that already holds a current SOC 2 or ISO 27001 report and has a live, named buyer questionnaire. This is operational drafting guidance, not legal, audit, certification, or security advice.

Never put authentication material in the pack

Do not include passwords, passkeys, private keys, API tokens, recovery codes, session cookies, production credentials, or buyer-portal sign-in details. Clearance Desk does not need them and does not submit the questionnaire for you.

Use a manifest before sharing files

List each proposed source by document title or ID, version or effective date, owner, relevant section, and the questionnaire rows it may support. The manifest lets the company reviewer remove irrelevant material before any drafting handoff.

Share the minimum authorized evidence

Prefer the relevant approved excerpt or redacted record when it supports the answer. Do not include customer data, employee records, payment-card data, government identifiers, health information, raw production data, or vulnerability details unless your company has independently determined that the specific disclosure is necessary, authorized, and handled through its approved secure process.

Separate policy from operating evidence

A policy can describe what should happen. A dated review record, exercise record, ticket, or approved system output may show what did happen. Label the source type accurately; do not treat a policy alone as proof that an activity occurred.

Preserve unsupported rows

If the authorized sources do not support a buyer question, mark the row unknown / not evidenced. Do not convert a missing record, ambiguous scope, or outdated document into a guessed “yes.”

Name the final company reviewer

The founder, CTO, CISO, counsel, or other authorized company reviewer decides what may be disclosed, corrects the draft, approves the final wording, and submits it. A drafting service cannot make or sign representations for the company.

A minimal handoff inventory

  • The exact named questionnaire file, with 100 questions or fewer.
  • The current report already held by the company and only the authorized supporting evidence.
  • An evidence manifest with document IDs, dates, owners, sections, and proposed row mappings.
  • A list of known gaps and stale sources that must remain visible.
  • The name and role of the company reviewer who will approve and submit the answers.

There is no upload form on this public preview. A real handoff cannot begin until payment, the named file, a complete authorized evidence pack, a named reviewer, and an available delivery slot are all verified.
