How to prepare a customer security questionnaire for an evidence-mapped draft

For a 30–200-person B2B SaaS team that already holds a current SOC 2 or ISO 27001 report and has one live, named customer questionnaire with 100 questions or fewer.

Confirm the request is ready

Before drafting starts, all five conditions must be true:

  1. The payment has been verified.
  2. The questionnaire is named and contains no more than 100 questions.
  3. The complete questionnaire file and authorized evidence are available.
  4. An authorized company reviewer is named.
  5. A delivery slot is free.

The five-business-day clock starts only after all five conditions are true. A promise that files will arrive later does not start the clock.

Build a safer evidence pack

Include only material your company is authorized to use for this response, such as:

  • Your current SOC 2 or ISO 27001 report.
  • Current approved policies and architecture notes.
  • Approved descriptions of access control, incident response, backups, business continuity, and vendor management.
  • Previously approved answers that are still accurate.

Do not send passwords, passkeys, API keys, private keys, recovery codes, session cookies, customer records, employee records, production data, vulnerability details, or evidence you are not authorized to share.

What an evidence-mapped draft should contain

  • A draft answer for each supported question.
  • A reference to the supplied evidence behind each answer.
  • Unknown / not evidenced wherever the supplied material does not support a claim.
  • A gap list for the authorized reviewer.
  • A reuse index for later questionnaires.

What drafting cannot do

  • It cannot create a security control that does not exist.
  • It cannot replace a CISO, auditor, certification body, or lawyer.
  • It cannot sign representations or submit through the buyer's portal.
  • It cannot guarantee that a buyer accepts the answers or closes a deal.
