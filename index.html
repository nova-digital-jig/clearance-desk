What it is

A drafting service. You send the named questionnaire and an evidence pack you choose (policies, the report you already hold, architecture notes, screenshots). We return a written draft answer packet mapped to that evidence, plus a gap list and a reuse index.

Missing evidence is marked unknown / not evidenced. We do not guess a yes.

What it is not

  • Not your CISO, security team, counsel, CPA, or auditor.
  • Not a signature on your controls.
  • Not a promise the buyer will accept the packet.
  • Not readiness, not a first SOC 2, not ISO body work.
  • Not a GRC platform.

Who it is for

CTO or founder at a 30–200 person B2B SaaS that already has SOC 2 or ISO 27001 in hand and has a live, named questionnaire in progress.

Pre-cert companies: hard no. Anyone who wants invented answers or a signature: no. Consumers and minors: no.

No. We are a drafting desk. We do not sit your audit. We do not hold production credentials.

No. We do not sign representations or certifications about your controls. You (or your CISO / counsel) stand behind whatever you submit.
